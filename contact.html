Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Essential guidelines for prospective athletes, parents, and school team coordinators.

We accept athletes starting from age 8 up to senior/open amateur divisions. Training groups are strictly divided by age and biological maturity: Sub-Junior (ages 8–12), Junior (ages 13–16), Youth (ages 17–19), and Senior Competitive squads. This ensures age-appropriate physical conditioning and safe contact intensity.

Complete beginners are warmly welcomed! Our Beginner Kabaddi and Beginner Kho-Kho programs teach fundamental footwork, body balance, diving technique, cant (breath control), and rules from ground zero. No prior competitive experience is necessary.

Players should arrive in breathable athletic shorts and an academy or moisture-wicking jersey. For Kabaddi, training on our cushioned mats is barefoot or in approved mat shoes, with knee and elbow compression sleeves recommended. For Kho-Kho, lightweight gum-sole agility shoes or barefoot sprint grip are standard. We supply all mats, sprint cords, hurdles, and medical ice baths.

Yes. We offer morning batches (06:00 AM - 07:30 AM), evening batches (05:30 PM - 07:30 PM), and dedicated weekend conditioning sessions. Students can request batch timing adjustments through the Player Dashboard if their academic exam timetables require temporary changes.

Athlete safety is our primary metric. All contact scrimmages take place on 40mm Olympic-grade shock-absorbent mats. Our facility employs a certified sports physiotherapist during active batch hours, maintains cryotherapy ice tubs for post-drill recovery, and adheres to strict concussion and joint sprain return-to-play protocols.

Every registered athlete receives access to the dedicated online Player Dashboard. Parents can monitor session-by-session calendar attendance logs, coach evaluation remarks, tournament registration rosters, and fee payment receipts 24/7 on any device.
