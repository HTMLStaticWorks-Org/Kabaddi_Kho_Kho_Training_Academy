Curriculum Breakdown

Six Structured Training Categories

Every tier builds progressive technical, physical, and tactical readiness.

01

Kabaddi Beginner

Basic raiding skills, defensive techniques, body balance, and AKFI rules & game fundamentals.

02

Kabaddi Advanced

Advanced raiding variations, defensive combinations, team strategies, and live match preparation.

03

Kho-Kho Beginner

Running fundamentals, dodging techniques, basic chasing skills, and spatial coordination.

04

Kho-Kho Advanced

Advanced chasing & pole dives, tactical movement, reaction speed & agility, and competitive practice.

05

Fitness & Conditioning

Strength training, endurance, flexibility, speed & agility, and injury prevention education.

06

Tournament Preparation

Match simulations, tactical planning, team coordination, and competition peaking readiness.
