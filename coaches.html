Learn From Coaches Who Understand The Game.

Our mentoring bench unites former national-level Kabaddi raiders, Kho-Kho speed tacticians, and strength specialists committed to disciplined athletic growth.

Six Core Values

Our Coaching Philosophy

Every practice block is anchored by six non-negotiable standards on and off the mat.

Discipline

Punctuality, mat etiquette, consistent warm-ups, and respect for officials and teammates.

Technical Excellence

Repetitive mastery of clean footwork, ankle holds, Kho deliveries, and safe pole-dive landings.

Physical Development

Age-appropriate strength, sprint acceleration, joint resilience, and injury-prevention habits.

Tactical Intelligence

Reading opponent formations, clock management, Do-or-Die raid selection, and chain timing.

Teamwork

Vocal mat communication, trust between corner and cover defenders, and selfless Kho switches.

Sportsmanship

Grace in victory, resilience in defeat, and lifelong pride in indigenous Indian sports culture.

Athlete Development Pathway

6-Step Training Methodology

How we take every player from initial evaluation to championship readiness.

01

Skill Assessment

Baseline evaluation of sprint speed, balance, flexibility, and current Kabaddi or Kho-Kho fundamentals.

02

Technical Training

Focused biomechanical drills for raiding touches, tackle grips, Kho tags, and zigzag footwork.

03

Physical Conditioning

Progressive agility ladders, core strength, anaerobic intervals, and mobility recovery blocks.

04

Tactical Practice

Situational squad scenarios, super-tackle traps, and video breakdown of real match footage.

05

Match Preparation

Referee-officiated practice matches with official clocks, scoring tables, and pressure drills.

06

Tournament Participation

District, university, and state championship competition backed by coach sideline analysis.
