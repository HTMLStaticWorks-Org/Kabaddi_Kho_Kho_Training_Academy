Our Core Training Sports

Dedicated arenas, specialized head coaches, and sport-specific performance tracks for both Kabaddi and Kho-Kho.

Mat Combat & Strategy Kabaddi

Kabaddi Mastery Wing

01

High-contact mat combat engineered around fearless breath-hold raids and synchronized 7-defender traps.

  • Raiding: Hand touch, toe-touch, dubki & bonus-line escapes
  • Defending: Corner block, cover dash & super-tackle discipline
  • Tackling: Ankle hold, thigh hold & double-arm chain traps
  • Team Coordination & Strength: Mat grip, core stability & cant endurance

Speed & Reflex Kho-Kho

Kho-Kho Speed Wing

02

Lightning-fast chasing and razor-sharp zigzag evasion across regulation 27m × 16m turf and mat courts.

  • Chasing: Explosive sitting-block launches & clean Kho hand-tags
  • Dodging: Single/double chain feints & ring defense patterns
  • Running Speed: 30m sprint mechanics & pole-turn deceleration
  • Quick Reflexes & Tactics: Pole dives, sky dives & timed-turn strategy
