Our Journey & Mission

Forging National Champions From Grassroots Talent

Founded by former national medalists, Raiders Sports Academy was established to give young Kabaddi and Kho-Kho athletes the world-class training environment they deserve. What began on outdoor mud courts has expanded into a championship sports complex equipped with international standard cushioned mats, synthetic courts, and biometric performance tracking.

Our mission is twofold: elevate Indian indigenous sports to global athletic prestige, and mold disciplined, confident young men and women who excel both on the competition mat and in daily life.

Foundational Pillars

The Four Pillars of Raiders Academy

Every drill, training batch, and match strategy is anchored in our four non-negotiable athletic standards.

01

Technical Mastery

Uncompromising focus on fundamental biomechanics: toe touches, bonus dives, thigh grips, ankle holds, pole turns, and zigzag evasion.

02

Relentless Discipline

Punctuality, respectful sportsmanship, mat etiquette, and academic balance. Our players represent their families and academy with honor.

03

Explosive Conditioning

Sport-specific metabolic conditioning, lactate threshold drills, core stability, and agility sprint ladders designed to outlast any opponent.

04

Tactical Intelligence

Understanding match flow, do-or-die raid strategies, chain coordination, clock management, and opposition video breakdown.
